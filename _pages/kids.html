--- layout: page title: Kids Stuff permalink: /kids/ --- ## Kids Stuff Since becoming a parent, I've been interested in finding ways to share ways to visualize numbers and other concepts with my son. I am sharing some of the things I've made here. ### [Kindergarten Apps](../apps/kindergarten-apps/index.html){:target="_blank"} A collection of apps I initially made for my son to help him practice some basic number skills. I am constantly thinking of more things I can add to this collection, and I have plans to make more apps in the future, as new ideas come to me. They are all free and open source, and you can find the code on [GitHub](https://github.com/{{site.github}}/kindergarten-apps){:target="_blank"}, released under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (you may modify it to suit your purposes, but it may not be used in commercial software or products).
