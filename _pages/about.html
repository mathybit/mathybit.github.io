--- layout: post title: About permalink: /about/ --- ## Basics Hi there! I'm Adi. I'm a mathematician and data scientist with a background in discrete mathematics, combinatorics, and graph theory. Over the years, my work has increasingly focused on artificial intelligence and machine learning, particularly applied deep learning. I tend to approach problems from a systems perspective: I'm interested not only in creating and training models, but in understanding how information flows through a system, how the different pieces interact. TLDR: I like turning interesting ideas into something I can use in the real world. ## Personal Interests ![Adi and David](../assets/img/profile.jpg){:class="image-left"} When I'm not working, I'm usually spending time with my son, David, or disappearing down one of my many hobby rabbit holes. Electronics (as in transistors, not iPads) have been a passion of mine since childhood. I enjoy designing and building audio electronics, and generally figuring out how things work. I love plugging my guitar into a preamplifier I built on the breadboard, and seeing the various sounds I can get from that. If you're into that stuff, check out [PyAmpScope](/pyampscope-1/), which I wrote to help me measure and characterize the circuits I build. I also like the arts, drawing, betta fish, and shooting arrows down the range, among other things. I make a really mean pizza from scratch. I'm a huge fan of the outdoors, biking, hiking, and generally being in nature.
